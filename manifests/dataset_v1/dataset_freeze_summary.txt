SPLIT METHODOLOGY
------------------------------
Caltech: camera-LOCATION-disjoint (not sequence-disjoint). Verified that every Caltech
sequence_id is unique per image (1,190 unique values for 1,190 images), so a
sequence-disjoint constraint would be a no-op. Verified that all 265/265 near-duplicate
pairs share the same camera location. Location-disjoint grouping therefore subsumes the
pair-grouping requirement AND protects against the model learning fixed-camera
backgrounds as a shortcut -- the primary reason for this choice.

iNaturalist: duplicate-group-disjoint (near-duplicate + exact-duplicate groups kept
together in one split).

SDXL: the 206 NOT_REVIEWED images are TRAIN-ONLY. Validation and test draw exclusively
from visually-reviewed KEEP images. EXCLUDE and NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW images are not in
the dataset at all. Held-out-generator data (for future out-of-distribution testing) is
separate from this split entirely and has not yet been selected.
